      How to read this screener

      Buy means the latest completed minute close is below P10; sell means it is above P90; otherwise neutral. Extended-hours bars are eligible. If no newer bar is available, the historical close is used. Before the first forecast session arrives, its first row is the comparison; the row date is always shown.

      Current versus closing signals

      Current signals are provisional, not trade instructions. Saved closing signals use the final observed minute of an exchange session (normally 4 pm New York time; earlier on early-close days). After-hours movement never changes that record. No closing signal is backfilled using a forecast generated after the close.

      Freshness and gaps

      Quote time, source, and forecast date are explicit. Missing quotes and expired forecast rows are not fabricated. Splits that invalidate a forecast require a new scan. The prior validated dataset remains published until the next scan passes its coverage checks.
