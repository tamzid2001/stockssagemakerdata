A few things to know

What gets billed?

With direct Quantura pay-as-you-go billing, a new custom ensemble forecast costs $0.50 when it completes successfully, whether requested on the website, through the API, or through MCP. The price includes the available models and Scout questions. Failed forecasts, cached results, status checks, screeners, historical downloads, and trial usage are not billed. 20 forecasts cost $10; 100 cost $50, before discounts and tax.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Try the full workflow, API keys, SDKs, and MCP server for 14 days. A payment method is required. Usage during the trial is free. Afterward, pay-as-you-go forecasts are billed monthly; Pro renews monthly or yearly at your selected price. One trial per account. Cancel before it ends to avoid charges.

How does WELCOME50 work?

Eligible new subscribers get 50% off their first paid billing period after the free trial: the first monthly usage invoice for pay as you go, or the first paid month or year of Pro. WELCOME50 appears automatically at checkout. Later billing uses the regular price, plus applicable taxes.

Can I control spending?

Your initial usage budget is $50 per billing month, before discounts and tax. Pending forecasts reserve $0.50 each. New paid runs stop when the budget is reached; failed runs release their reservation. Sign in to adjust the budget below.

Can I choose Pro instead?

Existing Pro subscribers keep their agreed monthly or annual pricing and features. Pro remains available to new customers too. No one is automatically switched to metered billing. Manage Pro in Clerk Account settings.

Can I subscribe through RapidAPI?

Yes. Choose Pay per Use or Pro on our RapidAPI listing. Marketplace billing, application keys and quotas are managed by RapidAPI. This subscription provides the published API endpoints; it does not activate a Quantura website plan or MCP login. Website trials, WELCOME50 and the website usage budget do not apply. Set marketplace usage alerts in RapidAPI.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Open Manage billing to cancel. Completed paid usage remains payable; saved results stay available according to your account access.

What can I forecast?

Supported stocks, crypto and perpetuals, prediction markets, and uploaded time series. Availability depends on the provider and the genuine history each selected model requires.
