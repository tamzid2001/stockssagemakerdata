About Quantura

Built for clarity when markets are noisy.

Quantura connects market data, probabilistic forecasting, screening, and private research workflows in one interface. Deterministic analysis appears first; optional AI interpretation remains user-controlled.

Why Quantura exists

Make research and forecasting transparent and auditable.
Preserve the P1/P25/P50/P75/P99 Quantura Forecast distribution rather than presenting one false-certainty target.
Keep credentials server-side and isolate private user workspaces.
Make AI interpretation optional, balanced, and secondary to the source data.

What the platform includes

Quantura Forecast multi-model distributions with deterministic quantile scenarios.
Alpaca and Yahoo Finance historical equity and options data workflows.
Prediction CSV validation, anomalies, model-bias heuristics, and date-aware boundary alerts.
Broad-universe quantitative screening and MLB Polymarket pregame datasets.
Private AWS role configuration for user-owned SageMaker execution.
Operating principles

Evidence, uncertainty, and user control

Quantura is a research platform, not a promise of trading outcomes. Source timestamps and missing fields stay visible, scenario percentages keep their true mathematical sign, and external infrastructure costs remain clearly separated from platform access.
