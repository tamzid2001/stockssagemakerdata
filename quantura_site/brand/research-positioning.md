# Quantura research positioning

The homepage now connects an audience's question to data, forecast ranges, and
research they can revisit. Its three entry points serve investors and traders
(screening), analysts and researchers (inspectable forecasts), and developers
(structured exports and protected APIs). Calls to action open existing product
features. Illustrations are labeled; no customer or performance claims were added.

The editorial introduction is shorter. Three featured guides cover reading a
range, checking the input data, and saving or exporting research. All 78 posts
have individually selected, distinct Unsplash photographs with photographer
credits. The registry and publication tools reject duplicate photos, including
the same underlying image with different resizing parameters.

Sources reviewed:

- https://bigdata.com/ and its workflow pages: adapt the emphasis on inspectable
  information and clear research outcomes.
- https://quartr.com/ and its product/API pages: adapt distinct audience entry
  points and the connection from research to developer workflows.
- https://docs.typesafe.ai/concepts/use-case-map#scientific-discovery: adapt
  explicit assumptions, reviewable evidence, and evaluation against observations.

These are positioning references. Quantura does not claim their proprietary data,
customer relationships, integrations, citation systems, or scientific capabilities.
