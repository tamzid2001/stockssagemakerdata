Prepare market data, upload prediction CSVs, run SageMaker Autopilot on daily history, and keep every result private in Explore until you publish it.

Quantura Forecast Foundry

Prepare a source

Historical downloader data is normalized to item_id, timestamp, and closing_price. Uploaded prediction CSVs are analyzed and pushed into Explore-ready request records automatically.

Forecast Foundry currently supports daily historical datasets only.
Foundry history preparation uses daily market data only.
Historical CSVs must resolve to item/date-close columns. Prediction CSVs should include quantiles such as P10, P50, and P90.

Autopilot controls

Pick a market-ready horizon. Forecast Foundry maps it to trading-day or trading-hour steps automatically based on your selected interval.
Enter 3 to 5 quantiles between 0.01 and 0.99. Use an odd count and include 0.5. Example: 0.1,0.5,0.9 or 0.1,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9.

Sign in with a full account to unlock Forecast Foundry.

Concurrent instance limit: 0 of 2 active.
Forecast Foundry currently supports daily historical datasets only. Prediction CSV uploads can still be analyzed here.

Research guide

Use the AWS Forecast metrics documentation to understand how Average wQL, wQL, WAPE, RMSE, MAPE, and MASE are interpreted. Lower error values indicate a stronger forecasting model.

In Forecast Foundry, the point-forecast anchor is the middle quantile column, which is usually the median P50 or 0.5.

For stock prediction analysis, Quantura takes the first actionable business-day median in the forecast window and the last valid business-day median in the forecast output, rounds both to the nearest 10, and compares them. If the last rounded median is below the first rounded median, Quantura selects the upper bound from the last business-day row as the point-side. Otherwise it selects the lower bound.
