## Weekly methodology

The pipeline runs **daily after the market closes** (22:15 UTC on weekdays).
“Weekly” describes the forecast horizon, not a once-a-week schedule.
It withholds one completed NYSE session and forecasts the next
**seven actual exchange sessions**. Prophet, Toto 4M, Granite, Chronos-2 and
TimesFM each receive a 20% central weight. Tail weights are renormalized across
capable models. There is no synthetic fallback. The prior validated scan remains
published until the new scan passes coverage. The website identifies the actual
published engine, including older scans during validation.

Prices use a split-adjusted basis. Later splits invalidate current comparisons
until refreshed. Current buy/sell signals mean strictly below P10 / above P90;
equality is neutral. The latest completed minute may include extended hours.
Historical-close fallback and comparison dates are labeled. Only the actual
exchange closing minute is saved as the closing signal, respecting early closes
and holidays. Previous non-neutral signals remain separate.

## Save filters and receive matches

Use any combination of minimum, maximum or average P01/P10/P25/P50/P75/P90/P99
across the seven sessions. Rules combine with AND. “Average P50 > 10% above price”
means `(average P50 - price) / price * 100 > 10`.

Open **Save filters & alerts**, sign in, and name the current filters. Optionally
enable daily email to your verified account address. Maximum ten filters.
Identical filters update their existing entry. Remove an entry to stop alerts.
Matches use finalized closing prices, not after-hours provisional quotes.
The inbox receives matches once per trading day; email-enabled matches are
combined into one email per account/day. Delivery limits apply. No location
permission is required. Provider acceptance does not prove inbox delivery.

```bash
curl https://quantura.studio/api/v1/me/screener-alerts \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY'

curl -X POST https://quantura.studio/api/v1/me/screener-alerts \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name":"P50 opportunity","email":false,"filters":{"signal":"buy","quantileRules":[{"quantile":"p50","statistic":"avg","operator":"gt","percent":10}]}}'
```

Reading requires `alerts:read`; saving/removing requires `alerts:write`.
Workspace Viewer status does not prevent managing personal saved filters.

## Open the published forecast

**View forecast** opens the exact published ticker forecast in Q Forecast without
new inference. **Save to profile** creates an immutable private copy including
history and model provenance; destination workspace write permission is required.
Old public scan links expire after publication replacement; saved private copies remain.

## Delivery operations

Brevo sends through a server-only Fixie proxy, with no direct-IP fallback.
The free Fixie plan allows 500 requests/month. Quantura budgets at most 450 per
billing window for all notification email and deduplicates sends durably.
Unknown delivery outcomes require reconciliation, not automatic retries.
