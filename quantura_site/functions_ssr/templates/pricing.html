Platform subscription vs. infrastructure charges

Quantura and third-party providers bill different parts of the workflow.

Quantura subscription

Covers the platform plan shown above, including its implemented screener-analysis allowance, collaboration seats, alert entitlement, and ad-free status.

AWS/SageMaker usage

When SageMaker runs through a user's connected AWS role, compute, storage, and related AWS services are billed separately by AWS to that user's account. SageMaker compute is not included in a Quantura subscription.

Market-data providers

Alpaca feeds, options coverage, rate limits, and historical availability depend on the configured provider account and its subscription or exchange entitlements.

Connecting an AWS role does not move billing to Quantura. SageMaker calls use the connected user's AWS identity where configured, so AWS charges remain in that AWS account.
